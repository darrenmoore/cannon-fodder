## Q1 -- should troops ever spawn mid-mission? (issue 001)

On the sink you get 6 men and that is the whole mission's supply -- there
are no reinforcements anywhere in the game, same as the original. You
expected more to spawn.

1. Keep it: your squad is all you get. Fix the *feedback* instead -- the
   briefing says the hold is 45s, the clock visibly pauses when you leave
   the zone, and the fail screen says the squad was the whole supply.
   (Recommended -- it is the original's design, and the sink was "really
   fun" even while starving.)
2. On hold/survive maps only, a couple of replacements walk in from the map
   edge between waves.
3. Give the sink a bigger squad (8) and leave the rule alone.

Recommend 1.

## Q3 -- how far must enemies start from the squad? (issues 010, 014)

No Way Off starts an enemy 3.6 tiles away -- inside his firing range before
you touch the mouse. Landing Ground: 6 tiles. There is no rule; hand-built
maps that thought about it used 11-12 tiles (just outside a veteran
rifleman's notice range).

1. 12 tiles minimum, every map, enforced by the checks. Enemies can still
   be close enough to reach you in seconds -- just not already shooting.
2. 9 tiles -- just outside fire range but inside notice range: you're seen
   immediately but have a beat to move.
3. 12 default, but a map header can lower it deliberately (for a map that
   *wants* a hot start).

Recommend 1 -- fixes every reported map, and nothing shipped today
deliberately wants a hot start.
