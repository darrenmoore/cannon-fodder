Everything here fell out of working the 200-qa brief: five research passes
over the sim, the renderer and every mission, plus reworking a dozen of them.
Each idea says where it came from, so none of this is taste -- it is what the
tree itself suggested while being worked on.

## How to read the ratings

**Effort** is honest sittings, not optimism:

| | |
|---|---|
| **S** | An hour or two. One commit. |
| **M** | One real sitting. |
| **L** | Several sittings, or one sitting plus a gauntlet round. |
| **XL** | A project. Needs its own brief. |

**Fun** is my guess at the payoff for a player, one to three stars -- and it
is a guess: the gauntlet rule applies to ideas too, and the person who thought
of one is the wrong judge of it. The short list I would actually start with:

1. **The daily patrol** (modes) -- S effort for something people come back for.
2. **Wading sound + the scream** (sound) -- the two loudest silences.
3. **Mud that pulls** (mechanics) -- makes the sink's hazard a hazard.
4. **End-of-mission stats** (polish) -- the numbers already exist, unshown.
