2 — size and crop

Alignment is the thing that makes it look like talking rather than lurching. Union crops every cell to one box measured across all of them — if the artist drew the two rows at different sizes, his whole head changes scale when he opens his mouth. Per cell measures each one separately and lines them up on a common anchor, which usually fixes it.

3 — nudge each frame

Each frame is shown with frame 0 ghosted over it in red. Anything that should not move — the cap, the badge, the eyes — should sit dead on top of its ghost. If it does not, nudge with the arrows, or click the frame and use ←↑↓→.
